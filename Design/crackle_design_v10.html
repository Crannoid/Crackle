Palette rationale — The shift from the original cool-indigo-black to a warm/cool contrast palette addresses a real-world usability problem: the original was too dark to read comfortably on a phone in daylight. The warm charcoal background and slate-blue surfaces are visually distinct without sacrificing the dark aesthetic.

Accent colour — Rust (#C8553D) replaced by Teal (#2DBCB0). Teal reads more clearly against the warm background and has stronger contrast against both the dark charcoal and the blue-grey surfaces. The rust accent was visually strong on the original indigo palette but lost impact against warm browns.

Previously used words — Words that have been Wordle answers are flagged with a strikethrough and "used" tag, dimmed to 60% opacity but not hidden. They remain valid guesses and hiding them without warning would silently reduce the user's options.

Suggestion list — max-height 370px shows approximately 10 items. 20 total suggestions are fetched — 10 visible, 10 accessible via scroll. A "↓ scroll for more" hint appears when more than 10 results are present. When valid words exceed 20, a "+ N more" message directs the user to add more constraints.
