Design Notes

Theme card anatomy. Three-colour swatch strip (BG, Surface, Accent) gives instant visual identity. Name and description. Radio circle — filled with accent tick when selected, empty otherwise.

Selected state. Card border switches to the theme's accent colour. Radio fills with that accent. Unselected cards recede to surface colour.

Settings screen inherits the active theme. Both mockups shown here use the theme they're selecting — the screen itself is a live preview.

Two other settings shown. Hide used answers (default off) and Show score numbers (default on). Both use inline toggle switches in the theme's accent colour.

Gear icon — Option 1. 6-tooth chunky rounded gear, chosen over the previous spoke-style icon. Active state uses --text-ui colour. Inactive uses --text-dim.
